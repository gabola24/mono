Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. What We Collect

  • Account data: Email address and authentication credentials via Clerk
  • Content: Messages, notes, references, plans, and habits you create
  • Usage data: Message counts, feature interactions, session analytics (PostHog)
  • Billing data: Subscription status managed by Stripe — we never see your card number
  • Error data: Crash reports and performance traces via Sentry

2. How We Use It

  • To provide and improve the Service (AI responses, mind graph, skill tree)
  • To manage your subscription and enforce usage limits
  • To detect bugs and prevent abuse
  • To send essential transactional emails (billing receipts, account alerts)

3. AI Processing

Your messages are sent to OpenAI's API to generate responses. OpenAI processes data per their Privacy Policy. We do not use your data to train AI models.

4. Data Storage

Data is stored in PostgreSQL on Railway (US region). Embeddings are stored using pgvector in the same database. Uploaded images are stored on Railway persistent storage.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Clerk — Authentication (Privacy Policy)
  • Stripe — Billing (Privacy Policy)
  • OpenAI — AI responses (Privacy Policy)
  • PostHog — Analytics (Privacy Policy)
  • Sentry — Error tracking (Privacy Policy)

6. Your Rights

  • Export: Download all your data via Settings → Export My Data
  • Delete: Permanently delete your account and all data via Settings → Delete Account
  • Correction: Contact us to correct inaccurate data

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all data is permanently removed within 24 hours.

8. Cookies

We use localStorage for session persistence and analytics preferences. No third-party advertising cookies.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

We will notify active users of material changes to this policy via email.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@zukuri.io
