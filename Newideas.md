## 1. PJ stats
Here the user can visualize itself in a summarized version of the skill tree. The goal is taking a quick look of the potential that they have in that moment and based on the last discovery.

## 2. Importance of thing. 
Here I want to have a way to capture when an idea, habit or concept is more important than others in some moments based on what the user is living. So the skill is more powerfull if to ask more about it, or show more work around it. The other way can be too, this mean if some time has passed since the last time you mention the topic, that skill will start loosing points. By this way the skill tree will not remain static and motivate the user to use the tool.

## 3. Capture the user flow. 

We need to take the first interaction as the most important of our tools. We want to gain the trust of the user, and capture the will to use our propose. 
I have some ideas here> Lets think in our creature, that lives inside our app. This creature is in a latent state, like sleeping, because he need ideas to live, he feeds with ideas. So the first interactions he might be ignoring you, in a funny way, until he makes you notice that he needs interaction about you, your ideas, your art, etc to live. i.e you presenet yourself and Once you give your name the create wake up more and start flowing the interaction in a more custom way.
